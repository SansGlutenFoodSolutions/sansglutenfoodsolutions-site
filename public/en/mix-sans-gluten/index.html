Gluten-free ORGANIC Universal Mix · 500 g

A versatile gluten-free mix, with just 5 ingredients.

Designed to serve as a basis for several preparations, the Food Solutions Gluten-Free Universal Mix combines a short formulation, certified ORGANIC, without corn or legumes, and French manufacturing.

Composition

5 ingredients. Nothing superfluous.

The communicated composition includes ORGANIC potato starch, ORGANIC white rice flour, ORGANIC tapioca starch, ORGANIC whole grain rice flour and ORGANIC psyllium. The proportions and manufacturing process are not published.

A common basis for several gluten-free recipes.

The Universal Mix is ​​developed for different preparations. The sheets will be published only after validation of the dosages and protocols.

Gluten-free pastry

The recipes of gluten-free and lactose-free pancakes, of gluten-free sponge cake and of gluten-free yogurt cake are now published. The yogurt cake uses 3 jars of Mix SGFS.
