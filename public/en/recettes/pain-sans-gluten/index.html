Gluten-free bread.

A small bun made into a bastard with 172 g of SGFS Mix, 163 g of water at 28°C and cooking in three phases on refractory stone to work on the volume, crumb and crust.

172 g of Mix SGFS≈ 350g raw dough26–28 °C for growth47–49 min cooking
See the recipeDiscover the mix

Ingredients

For 1 roll

  • Mix SGFS172 g
  • Water at 28°C163 g
  • Olive oil6 g
  • Salt3,5 g
  • Fresh yeast4 g
For steam

To expect 100 to 150 ml of boiling water, to be poured only into a metal container placed at the bottom of the oven. Do not pour water on the stone or the window.

Preparation

The step-by-step protocol

  1. 1

    Carefully mix the Mix SGFS and salt.

  2. 2

    Dissolve them 4 g of fresh yeast in the 163 g of water at 28°C.

  3. 3

    Pour the water + yeast onto the SGFS Mix. Mix 2 mins slowly, Then 4 min at medium speed with the leaf.

  4. 4

    Add the 6 g of olive oil during the last minute of mixing.

  5. 5

    Let it rest 15 min so that the psyllium contained in the mix hydrates and the dough holds together.

  6. 6

    With lightly oiled hands, very delicately shape a small bastard, without squeezing or degassing.

  7. 7

    Leave to grow 26–28°C for 1 hour.

  8. 8

    Just before cooking, perform a grune of 5 to 8 mm depth.

  9. 9

    Preheat the oven with refractory stone 45 min at 250 °C. Also place an empty metal container at the very bottom of the oven.

  10. 10

    Bake the bread on its baking paper, directly on the hot stone. Pour 100 to 150 ml of boiling water into the metal container and close immediately.

  11. 11

    Cook 12 min at 250°C. Then open the door briefly to release the steam, then continue 27 min at 220°C.

  12. 12

    Finish 8 to 10 min at 200°C, door slightly ajar, to dry the crust and crumb.

  13. 13

    Take out the bread, remove the baking paper and leave to cool on a rack at least 2 hours before cutting it.

Objective of this version

Obtain a more open crumb, with more visible cells, while maintaining good hold and avoiding a moist or gummy crumb.

Homemade gluten-free bread

Cooking on stone with steam.

This method combines controlled proofing, an oven blast at 250°C and a gentler end of cooking. Cooling for 2 hours is part of the protocol: the crumb continues to stabilize after leaving the oven.

Cooking mark

12 min at 250°C with steam, then 27 min at 220°C and finally 8 to 10 min at 200°C door slightly ajar.
