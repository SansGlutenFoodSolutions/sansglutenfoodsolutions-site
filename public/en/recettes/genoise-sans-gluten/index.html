For a simple cooking experiment, you can round up the basic recipe to 31 g of SGFS Mix et 31 g of sugar.

Preparation

The recipe step by step

  1. 1

    Preheat the oven to 180 °C.

  2. 2

    Mix the SGFS Mix with the baking powder.

  3. 3

    Separate the white from the egg yolk.

  4. 4

    Beat the egg white until very stiff with a small pinch of salt.

  5. 5

    Gradually add the sugar and continue beating for a few moments.

  6. 6

    Reduce the mixer speed and add the egg yolk.

  7. 7

    Immediately add the SGFS Mix and the baking powder.

  8. 8

    Mix very briefly so as not to cause the preparation to fall back.

  9. 9

    Pour immediately into a small mold and bake immediately.

  10. 10

    For this small format, start controlling the cooking from 10 to 12 minutes.

  11. 11

    Unmold while warm then let cool completely before slicing.

Volume mark

With the 1 egg recipe, a mold of around 10 cm would give approximately 3.5 to 4 cm thickness after baking. With the same quantity of dough: approximately 2.5 to 3 cm in 12 cm and 1.8 to 2.2 cm in 14 cm. These dimensions are practical estimates.

Gluten-free sponge cake recipe

This recipe combines SGFS Universal Mix, egg, sugar and a small quantity of gluten-free baking powder. The very firm whipped white provides volume: the final incorporations must therefore remain very brief to preserve the aeration of the preparation.

Frequently asked questions

Make a successful gluten-free sponge cake.

How much SGFS Mix do you need for 1 egg?

The basic recipe uses 31.25 g of Mix SGFS for 1 egg, with 31.25 g of sugar.

At what temperature should you cook the sponge cake?

Preheat the oven to 180°C and start checking the cooking of the small format from 10 to 12 minutes.

What diameter of mold to use?

The test sheet indicates only a small mold. As a guide, a round mold of around 10 cm should produce a sponge cake of around 3.5 to 4 cm thick. The calculator allows you to adapt the quantities to larger diameters.
