Frequently asked questions

About the Universal Mix.

What are the ingredients of the gluten-free ORGANIC Universal Mix?

The Mix is ​​composed of five ingredients: ORGANIC potato starch, ORGANIC white rice flour, ORGANIC tapioca starch, ORGANIC whole grain rice flour and ORGANIC psyllium. The proportions and manufacturing process are not published.

Is the Mix ORGANIC?

Yes. The Universal Mix is ​​certified ORGANIC and is made in a certified ORGANIC and gluten-free factory.

Does the Mix contain corn, legumes or additives?

No. The formulation is corn free, legumes free, added additives and preservatives added. It does not contain added xanthan gum, guar gum or HPMC.

What about the 14 allergens requiring notification?

The formulation was designed without the 14 European Union notifiable allergens. AFDIAG “barred ear” certification for the Mix is ​​planned before general marketing.

Are you studying projects in France and Europe?

Yes. Requests for paid samples, referencing, catering, distribution and professional partnerships can be studied anywhere in France and Europe. European projects are studied on a case-by-case basis depending on the country.

Is the Universal Mix 500 g already on sale?

Not yet on general sale. At this time, only paid samples are offered upon request. The marketing of the 500 g sachet is planned for the end of 2026 / beginning of 2027.

How much time should you allow?

Depending on the recipe, allow approximately 2 hours for the complete preparation. This time may vary depending on preparation, shaping, resting or possible proofing and cooking.

What recipes can you prepare with the mix?

Recipes for chocolate chip cookies, pancakes, sponge cake, yogurt cake and gluten-free bread are available. Other uses are panuozzo and pizza. Other applications may be added after validation.

Why are the recipes not yet available?

The cookies sheet is now published. The other sheets are still being developed and will be published when their dosages and protocols have been tested and validated.

Are samples free?

No. Samples offered before launch are chargeable. The price and terms are communicated according to request.
